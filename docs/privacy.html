1. Who we are

Pagelit is a free, ad-free PDF and Office document reader for Android, published by The356Company (nejiyoussef081@gmail.com). Pagelit is a local, offline-first app: the documents you open are read and annotated on your device, and they are never uploaded to us or to anyone else by Pagelit.

2. Information collected

We collect no personal information, and Pagelit does not include any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

2.1 Files and documents you open

When you open a PDF or Office document, Pagelit reads it locally on your device. Pagelit does not read or upload the contents of your file to any server. Highlights, underlines, strike-throughs, squiggly marks and sticky-note comments you add live in memory while the document is open, and can be written into a separate annotated copy of the PDF that you choose to save — that copy stays wherever you put it.

A short list of recently opened files and your reading progress (current page and total pages) are stored locally on your device through Android's shared preferences, so you can reopen documents and resume where you left off. Neither is uploaded anywhere. You can remove all of it at once from Settings → Clear recents and progress, or by clearing the app's data in Android settings.

2.2 Permissions the app asks for

  • Files / storage access — to open documents you pick or, if you optionally grant All Files Access, to list documents already on your device.
  • Photos / media — only when you save a page to your gallery. Pagelit writes that one image and nothing else.
  • Keep screen awake — an optional setting that stops the screen sleeping while a document is open. It collects nothing.

Pagelit requests no access to the internet, advertising ID, contacts, camera, microphone, precise location, or files other than those you explicitly open.

2.3 Information we do not collect

Pagelit has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not collect your name, email address, device identifiers, advertising ID, precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, or any other personal information. There are no ad networks, analytics SDKs, or crash-reporting SDKs in Pagelit.

2.4 Gallery exports and sharing

When you save a page to your gallery or share a document, Pagelit uses Android's system services. The destination app or folder you choose receives the file — its handling of that file is governed by that app's own privacy practices, not ours.

3. How we use information

The only use of local app data on your device is:

  • to operate the app offline (open, render, annotate and share documents);
  • to respond to your support email if you directly contact us.

4. Sharing with third parties

We do not sell, collect, or share your personal information with third parties.

Pagelit has no server, no database, and no third-party advertising or analytics SDKs; nothing you open or annotate is transmitted by us.

5. Children

Pagelit is not directed at children under 13 and is not a “mixed-audience” or kids app. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us (for example, by email), please contact us and we will delete that information.

6. Data retention & security

Recents and reading progress live only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app, clear its data, or use Settings → Clear recents and progress. We apply standard care measures commensurate with a 100% offline, local-only app.

7. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction you may have the right to:

  • access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you;
  • restrict, object to or withdraw consent for certain processing;
  • request portability of your data;
  • complain to your local data-protection authority.

Because Pagelit does not hold a data record of yours (we have no server and collect no data), all document data stays solely on your device (local recents, reading progress) where you control or delete it directly. Contact us at nejiyoussef081@gmail.com for anything else.

Residents of California (CCPA / CPRA). We do not collect, sell, or share personal information, and Pagelit contains no targeted advertising or third-party trackers.

Residents of the EEA, UK and Switzerland (GDPR / UK GDPR). Pagelit processes your documents strictly locally on your device without transmitting personal data to us or to third parties.

8. International transfers

Pagelit runs 100% locally and offline on your device and transfers no user data across borders or to remote servers.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published at this page with a new effective date. Material changes will be announced in the app where possible. Please review it periodically.
